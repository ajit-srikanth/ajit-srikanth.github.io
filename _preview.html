Ajit Srikanth

I am a PhD student at the University of Michigan, advised by Prof Dmitry Berenson.

Previously, I was an undergraduate researcher at the Robotics Research Center (RRC), IIIT-Hyderabad, supervised by Prof K. Madhava Krishna and in close collaboration with Prof Arun Singh. I spent my summer of 2025 working with Prof David Held at Carnegie Mellon University.

I am interested in robot learning for manipulation (and loco-manipulation). My long-term research goal is to work towards creating robust generalist loco-manipulators that can safely work with or assist humans with any tasks.

Projects

Articubot Extension

Extended a zero-shot sim-to-real policy for articulated object manipulation. Whole-body control, impedance control, and optimized execution pipeline.

Sound Source Localization

System combining semantic and temporal information to localize sound sources in videos via VLM-audio alignment.

ASAC — Spatial Audio

Auto-generating spatial audio (Dolby Atmos style) for movies using video understanding and active speaker detection.

Contract-NLI

Two-step BERT architecture improving NLI and evidence inference for legal contracts.

Omni-drive Robot

Mecanum wheel robot with depth cameras and onboard planning (CEM, MPPI, MPC) using ESDFs.

Klann Walker

8-legged Klann linkage walker: kinematics/dynamics analysis, laser-cut build, Dynamixel control.

Image Dehazing

Benchmarked haze removal methods; improved Dark Channel Prior with CLAHE preprocessing.

Object Reconstruction

3D reconstruction from monocular camera with ICP, pose graph optimization, and Bundle Adjustment.

Y86 Processor

Sequential and pipelined Y86 ISA Processor in iverilog with hazard handling and a basic compiler.
